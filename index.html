The Story

Why I'm building this.

Not the pitch. The real reason.

"Shopping started feeling like work. And I realized — I liked making money more than spending it. But every purchase still felt like draining, not investing. Until I started understanding how business and money actually work."

I grew up watching women work incredibly hard — in male-dominated workforces, in systems that weren't built for them, often without the support structure that men take for granted. Men get to have a partner at home. Women are expected to do it all, alone, and still show up.

The result? Women spend to cope. They shop as a reward for the grind. They drain their accounts on brands that make them feel good for a moment — because no one ever showed them how to OWN those brands instead.

I know because I was that woman. I was great at selling. I generated $450K in four months knocking on doors in the summer heat. I understood business on the outside. But my own money? I was consuming, not building.

That's the frame I'm working to change. And the work I'm building to change it.

When I was interviewing successful women for a role at NWM — women who had made it professionally — none of them fully understood their own finances. That wasn't a personal failure. That was a systemic one. And it confirmed everything I was building toward.

"From Consumer to Owner isn't just a product name. It's the shift I made — and the one I'm building for every woman who's been draining instead of building."

What D2D Actually Teaches You

01
Territory as a Market
Map before you walk. Identify density, skip problem addresses, prioritize high-intent signals. Your territory is an ICP waiting to be segmented.
02
Trust Before Pitch
The pest control pitch that works isn't about bugs. It's about protecting their home, their family, their investment. Lead with what they care about.
03
Objection = A Trust Question
"Too expensive" is never about price. Every objection is a question about whether they believe you. Answer the real question.
04
Emotional Regulation IS a Sales Skill
You still have to knock the next door with the same energy as the first — after 20 rejections in the summer heat. That's the real skill.

"The close is the least important moment in the sale. The trust you built in the 4 minutes before it is everything."

The Startup Concept

From Consumer
to Owner.

A women-focused investing education platform. The problem is real. The audience is massive. The GTM is built. I am the market.

The Problem

Women drive 80% of consumer spending. They work in systems not built for them. They spend to cope. They drain rather than build. No investing platform was ever built for her first — and it shows.

The Insight

I interviewed successful professional women for a role at NWM. None of them fully understood their own finances. This isn't a personal failure. It's a systemic gap — at every income level.

The Solution

Teach investing through brands she already loves. Stocks through Lululemon. Bonds through Starbucks. The Fed through why her latte costs more. Familiar = accessible. Accessible = action.

Community + Events

Polished.

A community for women who work hard and want to build something that lasts. Starting in Venice, CA.

"Women don't need more conferences with name tags. They need more rooms where they can speak honestly — and leave with something real."

Polished is the community layer underneath From Consumer to Owner. It's where women founders, operators, and builders come together in Venice and beyond — not to network, but to actually build alongside each other.

The format is intimate: 12–20 women, a guided conversation, warm introductions from the host, and one real follow-up action per attendee. No pitch decks. No panels. Just women doing the work, together.

This is also GTM proof — building community IS the go-to-market motion for modern startups. Every event is lead generation, trust building, and distribution simultaneously.
